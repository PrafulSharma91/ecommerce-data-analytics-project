# E-Commerce Sales & Customer Analytics

An end-to-end Data Analytics project exploring Brazilian e-commerce data to identify sales trends, customer behaviour, delivery performance, and business growth opportunities.

## Project Overview

- **Dataset:** Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset
- **Orders Analyzed:** 96,470 delivered orders
- **Total Revenue:** BRL 15.42 Million
- **Tools:** Python, Pandas, NumPy, SQL, SQLite, Scikit-learn, Google Sheets, Looker Studio

## Tech Stack & Workflow

- **Python & Pandas:** Data cleaning, merging, data auditing, and feature engineering.
- **SQL & SQLite:** Business analysis using JOINs, CTEs, aggregations, and window functions.
- **Machine Learning:** RFM analysis and K-Means customer segmentation.
- **Data Visualization:** Matplotlib, Seaborn, and Looker Studio.
- **Dashboard:** Google Sheets and Looker Studio for interactive reporting.

## Key Business Insights

- Generated BRL 15.42M in delivered revenue with an AOV of BRL 159.83.
- November 2017 recorded peak monthly revenue of BRL 1.15M.
- Top 3 states (SP, RJ, MG) contributed 62.54% of total revenue.
- Late deliveries had a significantly lower average review score (2.27 vs. 4.29).
- Repeat purchase rate was only 3%, highlighting a customer retention opportunity.
- RFM analysis identified high-value customers at risk of becoming inactive.

## Business Recommendations

- Launch targeted win-back campaigns for high-value inactive customers.
- Improve logistics performance in states with higher delivery delays.
- Introduce post-purchase offers and loyalty campaigns to improve repeat purchases.

## Interactive Dashboard

The project includes a 3-page Looker Studio dashboard:

1. **Executive Summary:** Revenue, orders, AOV, and delivery performance.
2. **Sales Performance:** Product category and state-wise analysis.
3. **Customer Analytics:** RFM segmentation and customer insights.
